Based on the Vision component maturity matrix in Digital Adoption Tool v5.0.xlsx, the actions below are written as practical activities that a Change Manager could undertake to move from each maturity stage to the next. These actions align directly to the expectations in the matrix.
Vision: Not Started (0) → Emerging (1)
Strategic Direction & Leadership Lens
1.	Facilitate an initial vision workshop with the SRO, sponsors, and key programme stakeholders to explore the future state.
2.	Gather information on organisational priorities, programme objectives, and expected outcomes to inform vision development.
3.	Document initial themes describing why the change is needed and what success may look like.
4.	Identify key leaders who need to contribute to and endorse the vision.
5.	Establish a process and timeline for drafting the vision statement.
People Experience & Culture Lens
1.	Conduct stakeholder listening sessions to understand current challenges and expectations.
2.	Gather feedback on people's perceptions of the proposed change.
3.	Identify common concerns, aspirations, and opportunities that should influence the vision.
4.	Create simple awareness messages explaining that a vision is being developed.
5.	Ensure representation from key stakeholder groups in early vision discussions.
 
Vision: Emerging (1) → Developing (2)
Strategic Direction & Leadership Lens
1.	Draft a formal vision statement describing the future state and intended outcomes.
2.	Test the draft vision with senior leaders to ensure strategic alignment.
3.	Refine the vision using feedback from sponsors and governance groups.
4.	Validate how the vision aligns with organisational objectives and programme benefits.
5.	Develop a high-level vision narrative that can be used consistently across the programme.
People Experience & Culture Lens
1.	Run workshops with impacted stakeholder groups to test understanding of the draft vision.
2.	Collect structured feedback on whether the vision feels relevant and meaningful.
3.	Identify gaps or ambiguities in the vision from a staff perspective.
4.	Update the vision to reflect stakeholder insights and concerns.
5.	Begin developing people-centred messaging explaining how the vision affects different stakeholder groups.
 
Vision: Developing (2) → Embedding (3)
Strategic Direction & Leadership Lens
1.	Secure formal approval of the vision through programme governance.
2.	Develop a leadership communication plan to support vision rollout.
3.	Equip sponsors with key messages, presentations, and briefing materials.
4.	Integrate the vision into programme governance documentation and reporting.
5.	Ensure leadership communications consistently reference the agreed vision.
People Experience & Culture Lens
1.	Develop audience-specific communications explaining what the vision means for different groups.
2.	Deliver engagement sessions to build understanding and awareness.
3.	Establish feedback channels to assess comprehension and acceptance.
4.	Use change agents and sponsors to reinforce the vision locally.
5.	Monitor stakeholder understanding through surveys, workshops, or feedback sessions.
 
Vision: Embedding (3) → Adopted (4)
Strategic Direction & Leadership Lens
1.	Ensure sponsors consistently communicate the vision during governance and operational meetings.
2.	Integrate the vision into programme decision-making and governance discussions.
3.	Review leadership communications regularly for alignment and consistency.
4.	Provide coaching to sponsors and leaders on vision-led leadership behaviours.
5.	Measure leadership engagement in promoting the vision.
People Experience & Culture Lens
1.	Deploy a formal vision communication campaign across all impacted stakeholder groups.
2.	Establish regular feedback loops to assess understanding and engagement.
3.	Monitor stakeholder awareness and adjust communications where necessary.
4.	Use case studies and practical examples to demonstrate the vision in action.
5.	Reinforce the vision through team meetings, departmental briefings, and change network activities.
 
Vision: Adopted (4) → Thriving (5)
Strategic Direction & Leadership Lens
1.	Embed the vision into organisational planning, governance, and decision-making processes.
2.	Ensure leaders consistently reference the vision when making strategic and operational decisions.
3.	Review organisational priorities regularly to ensure continued alignment with the vision.
4.	Use adoption and performance data to refine and strengthen how the vision is communicated.
5.	Establish mechanisms to continuously evaluate whether organisational behaviours remain aligned to the vision.
People Experience & Culture Lens
1.	Measure how well staff understand and can articulate the vision.
2.	Capture and share success stories demonstrating delivery of the vision.
3.	Encourage teams to identify local improvements that support achievement of the vision.
4.	Maintain continuous engagement and listening activities to keep the vision relevant.
5.	Reinforce the vision through recognition programmes and celebration of achievements.
6.	Ensure the vision becomes part of everyday conversations, team objectives, and organisational culture.
