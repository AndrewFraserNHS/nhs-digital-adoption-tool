TARGETED REINFORCEMENT ACTIONS

actions.
FIXED PAIN POINTS

ONGOING BENEFITS

reinforcement is needed.

FALLEN ADOPTION
Adoption falls after initial friction because no one closes the loop.

Small irritants become entrenched behaviours and workarounds.
UNPROVEN BENEFITS
Benefits remain unproven and scaling loses momentum.
OLD HABITS RETURN
Teams revert to previous ways of working when AVT in not embedded
into daily routines.
ONE OFF TRAINING
Staff receive go live training but no refresher support as context changes.

WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE
RELIABLE AVT USE
Users can use AVT reliably in real settings and maintain full clinical
responsibility through “Human in the Loop” review and approval.
CHANGE CHAMPIONS SUPPORT

skills.

reinforcement and clearer workflows.

use.

RISKS IF YOU DON’T DO THIS
STALLED ADOPTION
Early trust issues spread socially and adoption stalls.
UNSAFE PRACTICES
Unsafe practice arises when “Human in the Loop” review and approval
and clinical accountability are not embedded.
VENDOR DEPENDENCY
Organisations remain dependent on vendors for day-to-day optimisation.
GENERIC TRAINING
Support does not reflect different roles, workflows or confidence levels.
LOW NFIDENCE

WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE
STRUCTURED READINESS
Structured readiness criteria are used before scaling.
USEFUL LEARNING CAPTURED
Learning is captured and reused through evidence and examples.
REPEATABLE CHANGE PRACTICES
Change practices become repeatable, with clear ownership, cadence and
measures.
LEADERSHIP ALIGNMENT
Senior leaders are aligned on why AVT matters and what success look
like.
BENEFITS ARE OWNED
Expected benefits are clearly defined, tracked and owned beyond go live.
RISKS IF YOU DON’T DO THIS
INCONSISTENT CHANGE
Change becomes reactive and inconsistent; fatigue increases as lessons
are not implemented.
EVIDENCE LED DECISIONS
Scaling decisions become enthusiasm driven rather than evidence led.
GOVERNANCE GAPS
Governance and readiness gaps create avoidable disruption and loss of
trust.
ADOPTION NOT ACTIVELY MANAGED
Focus stays on go live, with limited tracking of usage, confidence or
barriers.
INSUFFICIENT CHANGE CAPABILITY
Teams lack the time, resource or skills to support adoption properly.

Celebrating Success
Map with pin with solid fill
Success stories should reinforce adoption behaviours, benefits realised and improvements delivered through AVT.
WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE
PROGRESS IS MADE VISIBLE
Achievements are shared clearly so people can see what has improved
and why it matters.
USEFUL LEARNING CAPTURED
Celebrating success includes reflecting on what worked well, so the
learning can be used again.
PEOPLE RECOGNITION
Recognise the people behind the success, including quieter contributions
and team effort.
SUCCESS MEASURES DEFINED
Clear definitions for measuring success is shared and understood.
TIMELY RECOGNITION
Success is acknowledged soon after it happens, so staff feel their
contribution has been noticed and valued.
RISKS IF YOU DON’T DO THIS
MOTIVATION DROPS
People may feel their effort goes unnoticed, which can reduce energy,
commitment and enthusiasm.
PROGRESS BECOMES INVISIBLE
Without celebration, improvements and achievements can be overlooked
or forgotten.
LEARNING IS LOST
If AVT does not pause to recognise success, it may also miss the
opportunity to understand what worked well.
CONFIDENCE IS REDUCED
Staff may be less likely to take initiative or try new approaches if success
is not acknowledged.
TEAM SPIRIT WEAKENS
A lack of recognition can reduce shared pride, connection and
collaboration across AVT.
