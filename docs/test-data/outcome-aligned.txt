Vision Outcomes
Outcome 1: A Compelling Future State Has Been Defined
Outcome Statement
A clear, credible and compelling vision of the future state has been developed and aligned with the organisation's strategic objectives and desired outcomes. 
Measures
•	Approved vision statement exists.
•	Vision formally signed off through governance.
•	Vision aligns to documented strategic objectives.
•	Stakeholders agree the vision is clear and meaningful.
•	Leaders can describe the future state consistently.
This outcome answers:
"Do we know where we are trying to get to?"
Outcome 2: The Vision Is Understood and Shared
Outcome Statement
Stakeholders understand the purpose of the change and can articulate the vision, intended outcomes and benefits in a consistent way. 
Measures
•	% of stakeholders who can explain the vision in their own words.
•	% of stakeholders who understand why the change is happening.
•	Reduction in conflicting interpretations of the vision.
•	Survey score for understanding of change purpose.
•	Feedback demonstrates common understanding across stakeholder groups.
This outcome answers:
"Do people understand where we are going and why?"
Outcome 3: The Vision Is Visible and Guides the Change
Outcome Statement
The vision is consistently reinforced by leaders and change agents and is used to guide decision-making, communications, priorities and programme activity. 
Measures
•	% of leadership communications referencing the vision.
•	Communication audits show consistent use of agreed messaging.
•	Sponsors and change champions communicate aligned messages.
•	Programme decisions demonstrate alignment to the vision.
•	Governance papers, plans and communications reference the vision.
This outcome answers:
"Is the vision actually influencing behaviour and decisions?"
