SENIOR SPONSORSHIP – page 1 of 2

WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE
CLEAR SPONSORSHIP IN PLACE
Clear credible sponsorship in place – named SRO, clinical sponsor (e.g.
Medical Director/CCIO) and operational lead with ownership beyond
digital/IT.
VISIBLE LEADERSHIP
Visible leadership driving change on the ground – actively engaging
teams, reinforcing new behaviours and removing barriers to adoption.
OPERATIONAL OWNERSHIP
Operational ownership embedded early and leading workflow redesign.
FRONTLINE FEEDBACK
Frontline feedback is heard and acted on.
REGULAR REVIEWS
Senior leaders regularly review progress, risks and benefits.
RISKS IF YOU DON’T DO THIS
FAILURE TO SCALE
Without senior clinical and operational leadership, adoption depends on
individual enthusiasts and fails to scale across services.
NO HUMAN IN THE LOOP
Workflow issues and new ways of working, including “Human in the Loop”
review and approval, may not be owned or resolved.
SLOW UPTAKE
Without visible senior backing, teams are less confident to change
practice, delaying adoption and limiting impact.
DELAYED DECISION MAKING
Decisions are delayed because ownership is unclear.
INCONSISTENT MESSAGING
Leadership messages are inconsistent across services.

GOVERNANCE – page 1 of 2

The Playbook explains governance readiness requirements. This guidance provides practical considerations for establishing effective governance.
WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE
CLEAR GOVERNANCE
There is a clear AVT governance forum aligned to existing clinical and
operational structures.
CLEAR DECISION RIGHTS
Decision rights are clear and defined (who decides and owns what across
IG, IT, clinical and operational teams).
CLEAR ESCALATION ROUTES
Clear escalation routes ensure risks, behavioural challenges and adoption
issues are raised and acted on.
SAFE DELIVERY
Governance enables safe delivery rather than blocking progress
DOCUMENT DECISION RIGHTS
Decision rights are documented: who can approve, pause or reject.
RISKS IF YOU DON’T DO THIS
NO GOVERNANCE
Without governance, there is no clear decision making between IG, IT and
clinical/operational teams which may lead to delays.
HIDDEN RISK
Risks may remain hidden or unmanaged leading to workarounds and
further risks.
TRANSITION TO SCALING UP
Without proportionate governance, pilots don’t transition into controlled
organisation wide roll outs.
MONITORING
There may not be any ongoing monitoring after deployment.
RECURRING ISSUES
Issues will recur because root causes are not addressed.
